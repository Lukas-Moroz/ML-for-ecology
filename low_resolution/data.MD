## Raw Data 
honolulu_zoning: 
- GIS zoning data from HIGIS for the Honolulu county (Oahu) 

oahu: 
- Historical landsat data from NASA on the spectral bands reflected and gathered for Oahu (2014 - 2024)

oahu_landsat7: 
- Historical landsat data from NASA on the spectral bands reflected and gathered for Oahu (2000-2013)
